## Decisions (made once; binding)

1. **Beginner support is one system** (from findings-learnability.md section b): a Learn view (`#learn`: concepts with meaning, where you see it, how to read it, common mistake, tiny diagrams for degree, betweenness, closeness, clustering, constraint; a "find it in the app" task index; worked examples), an **Explanations: on/off** switch (default on for first visits; one localStorage preference wrapped in try/catch), a `Term` component (dotted underline, opens on click/tap, links to Learn), a `HowToRead` disclosure (what it means, what is big/small, one sentence from the live data, one common mistake), purposeful empty states (`NeedsData({ title, purpose, shows })` with "or explore the sample"), and a "Who stands out" block on Network.
2. **Navigation order** (keep labels, add a one-line description per view shown in the phone menu and as desktop title): Get a network: Data · Build · Generate | Explore: Network · People · Groups · Content · Time | Report: Methods & Export · Ask | Learn. Views that cannot apply to the loaded data stay as links with a muted "no text" / "no dates".
3. **Start page**: three equal choices before the drop zone — "Draw or type a small network" (Build), "Explore a sample organization" (loads it), "Analyze your own exports" (import) — then "New to network analysis? Learn the ideas".
4. **Names**: undirected networks show "Contacts (degree)"; directed show "Contacts" and "Total ties (in + out)"; never a bare "Degree" without qualifier anywhere (People, Time, summary, exports, appendix). Closeness is labeled "Closeness (harmonic)" with the formula in plain words and how it differs from the textbook 1/sum of distances. Betweenness says it is normalized (share of pairs) and how to get the raw count. Content tab "Affect" becomes "Tone". Diffusion "seed" becomes "first user". The masthead "Start over" becomes "Clear loaded data"; builders say "New ...". Communities are numbered from 1 everywhere, including every export. The generator's department called "People" is renamed (e.g. "People Operations") so it never collides with the People view.
5. **Statistics read verdict-first**: one plain sentence, then the number in plain words, then details. When p is at its floor say "none of the N random networks came this close (p <= 1/(N+1))". One replicate count per test across all views (200), and a test run once is reused by every view so numbers agree (N3). The modularity null re-runs community detection on each rewired network (N2) — the current fixed-partition null is replaced. Rank-stability wording is plain ("Settled: stays at rank 1 in all resamples") and carries the caveat that resampling events cannot test whether ties exist (N14); flag ties at displayed precision (J6) and let the student choose top k.
6. **Interpretation guards against overclaiming** (Jordan): attributes are snapshots — say so wherever a group-based time series or before/after uses an attribute (J1); when Time has detected a shift, Groups shows a banner with E-I / cross-group share before vs after (J2) and Compare includes E-I; shift persistence is described against the pre-shift baseline (J3); shifts recompute when the window changes (J4, bug); people with a detected departure or deactivation are marked in People and the profile (J7); fragility evidence (top-k betweenness share; "remove these people" what-if) in Network or People (J8).
7. **Survey correctness** (Maya, class): a valued tie field (1..N) on a roster relation becomes the tie weight by default and is exported (C1); in stitched ego surveys, perceived alter-alter ties are excluded by default and shown as a separate relation (C2); the ego interview respects "every tie counts 1" for ego measures (C4); the perceived-network builder has an undirected toggle (default on for friendship-like relations) that mirrors ticks and is carried to analysis, and informants whose symmetry differs are flagged (M1, M2).
8. **Reports and exports are complete and honest**: the methods appendix lists every analysis actually run with its parameters and key results (null means, z, p, intervals; diffusion, before/after, shift detection), no internal keys (N4, N5, N6, N7, N12, N23, C10); the summary report includes the null-model and group-mixing results (N4, N13); exports carry a `contacts` column, consistent edge attribute names (`evidence_<rule>` everywhere), and an option, on by default, to leave out contact details (email, handles, platform ids) (N10, N11, N19).
9. **Recovery check** uses the current construction settings (with a note) so A8 can compare constructions (N24); names the planted brokers with their measured ranks (J5); one stated verdict rule across checks (J9); plain wording with technical terms in parentheses (L11, N15); explains why diffusion recovery (true ties) can differ from the Diffusion view (observed ties) and warns when the shuffled baseline leaves little room (N8).
